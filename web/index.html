    How it works

    Warbler filters the answer list by every color you report, shortlists guesses by letter and position frequency (favoring letters that split the pool, avoiding repeats early), then ranks the shortlist by expected information, with a small bonus for guesses that could be the answer. Everything runs in your browser.

    No data leaves your device.
